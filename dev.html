Dev tools

Every test and tuning page. The dev server and the beta have them all; the live game has none.

Start here

  • QA deskqa.html

    A bench for each thing to try out, live, in every state that matters: the pieces on their own (the avatar, the daily streak, the descent, the rune circles), with their controls, and every scene of the game. / finds one; docs/qa.md has the rest. The streak at 30 days

Delve

  • The lablab.html

    The real game screen beside a panel that sets up and plays a Delve run by hand: players, depth, cards, questions, events, end screens, spatial sound.

  • Backdropsbackdrop.html

    Pick Delve's backdrops by hand: depth, brightness, each zone's look, the generator, the endgame strata. Saves to src/data/backdrops.json on the dev server.

  • Zone cardzones.html

    Tune the zone title card in place: step into any zone, compare families, freeze a frame, phone width.

  • Card benchcards.html

    Any category card, plain or with a find (or the dig's way out), up to three pinned and dealt on the real screen: hover them, click one to turn it over, deal again; phone width.

Wraeclast

  • Wraeclast runwraeclast.html

    The new mode's playable prototype (v5): a solo run in an open region of 14 places, entered in any order from the camp. Each zone holds one item, its name built from tiles with Wordle marks and its art under a veil, and naming it spreads the Seed of Corruption a ring out from its lair. Three towns heal, sell four supplies and offer a boon; the lair's duel is open from the start. Its tuning panel sits at the page's foot; the dev server keeps each run's log in .wraeclast-logs (scripts/wraeclast-log-summary.mjs sums it up). Rules in docs/wraeclast.

Sound

  • Spatial soundspatial.html

    The game screen full window with a menu for tuning the spatial sound on any sound. M hides the menu.

  • Hover repeatshover.html

    How the hover sound quietens over a sweep of the mouse across controls.

  • Sound levelslevels.html

    Every sound, and a few that land together, through the game’s mix with its lift switched between before and after; blind if you like.

Rooms

  • Room botbot.html

    Made-up players that open or join rooms and play a while. Dev server only (or npm run bot); the beta doesn't build it, so it has no card there.

Analytics

  • Analyticsstats.html

    The game's counts from GoatCounter, read with the owner's API key, which stays in this browser. Unlike the tools above it is in the live game too (poe2.quest/stats.html), linked from nowhere. ?demo fills it with made-up numbers, no key needed; #board is the overview for a monitor of its own; #groups sets a country, a device or a mode's players against everyone.
