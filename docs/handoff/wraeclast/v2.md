# Handoff: Wraeclast run, prototype v2 (after playtest 1)

The solo test page `wraeclast.html` exists from v1. This handoff changes it
after the owner's first playtest, which found the run too easy and badly
balanced. Rework the page in place; keep what still fits (run log, tuning
panel, life and flask, the camp and town flow).

Full design (owner only): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0fa0afec-10a6-450a-873f-0600974437b6

This file is the source of truth for the prototype's rules, numbers and data.
Where the design doc says more (the map, boons, co-op, tiers), leave it out.
Every rule in `CLAUDE.md` applies (no em dashes, phones from 375 px, plain
`:hover`, `src/lib/storage.ts` for storage, generated data never hand-edited).

## What playtest 1 found, and what changes

| Found | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Flares beat everything; one lifted patch often names the item | Art is scarce: no free art in most zones, and a Flare burns in only a small part at a random spot |
| Requirement and lore reveals help almost nobody | Both are gone |
| Some mod lines give the item away | The Scroll of Wisdom only shows common lines (shared by 3 or more items) |
| Nothing works on the Inscription itself | Four of the six supplies now work on the Inscription |
| Syllables don't join into words | The answer line spaces words by itself, without confirming the answer |
| Some tiles were whole words, which made names easy | Finer syllables: no tile is a whole word except "of", "the", "and"; all lower case |
| Boards of only bows spoil the item type | Boards mix categories: about half the names from other categories |
| Category cards spoil the type, and multipliers without a cost are always taken | Category cards are gone. Rewards are loot drops, and the risk is corruption |
| The free clue alone is often enough | Free clues are weaker, and one zone type has none |
| The shop doesn't feel like a shop | Wares are dealt as cards with prices (see Look) |

## Scope

In:
- One solo run of 15 items: a camp, 4 zones of 3 items, a town between zones
  2 and 3, then a boss chain of 3 under the Silent rule (only Flares work).
- The Inscription with mixed boards and auto spacing; the burn-in Veil; the
  free clue; the six supplies; loot deals with one unidentified card; Vaal
  Orbs that corrupt items; life and the flask; the camp and town vendor.
- The two generated data files, updated, with their tests.
- The run log and tuning panel, updated.

Out: the map, boons, events, Wells, tiers, co-op, resume after a reload, the
Codex and achievements. Remove the category card pick and its multipliers.

## A run

1. **Camp.** 100 life, a flask with 2 charges, 20 gold, an empty pack of 8
   slots. The vendor deals every ware (table below).
2. **Zones 1 to 4,** 3 items each. Each rolls a zone type (table below); the
   four are all different. Nothing is used while travelling: each phase
   asks one decision (travel: where; town: what to buy; item: what to spend
   and what to answer; loot: which card).
3. **Town** between zones 2 and 3: heals 20 life on arrival; the vendor deals
   every ware again.
4. **Boss,** Silent: 3 items with 10 names each, only Flares work. Item 1
   starts with a free burn-in, item 2 with nothing, item 3 with nothing and
   Flares burn in half as much. No loot.
5. The run ends at 0 life (a loss) or after the boss (a win), on the end
   screen with the run summary and Copy run log.

## An item

1. **The item opens** with the Inscription, the Veil (the whole art
   covered) and the zone's free clue.
2. **Use supplies** from the pack, a Vaal Orb (see Corruption) or a flask
   charge, at any moment of the item. Nothing can be bought during an item.
3. **Build the name.** Tap a syllable to place it; tap a placed one to take
   it back; Clear empties the line. A syllable can be placed more than once.
4. **Submit.** Every submit counts, also a build that is no real name.
   - **Right:** the loot deal (below). Then the next item.
   - **Wrong:** life drops by the miss cost, the coming deal loses a card,
     and if the build was one of the board's names, that name is struck.
     The player keeps trying.
5. No skip. An item never comes up twice in a run.

### Board

| Items | Names |
| --- | --- |
| Zones 1 and 2 | 4 |
| Zones 3 and 4 | 6 |
| Boss | 10 |

Blight adds 2 names, a Twisted corruption 3. Never more than 10; Name
strike never leaves fewer than 3.

**Picking the wrong names** (replaces `Engine.makeQuestion` for this mode):
- Half the wrong names (rounded down) from the item's own category, half
  from all other categories.
- Within each half, prefer names that look alike: rank candidates by shared
  syllables with the answer (from `syllables.json`), then by closeness in
  syllable count, and draw from the top 15 at random so boards vary.
  `src/lib/lookalikes.ts` may seed the same-category half.
- No made-up names from `fakes.json`.

**The tiles:** the distinct syllables of all names on the board, shuffled,
lower case, each once.

**Struck names:** a syllable no remaining name uses is greyed out (by a miss,
a Name strike or a Decoy sweep).

### Auto spacing

The answer line inserts a space before a placed syllable when that syllable
starts a word in any name on the board. Syllables that start words in some
names and not others (common with mixed boards) always get the space, so the
line never confirms the answer, and wrong words can still be built.
Tuning toggle: spacing on or off.

### The Veil

No grid. Reuse the Delve veil pipeline: organic patches cut by
`src/lib/patches.ts` and burned in with `src/lib/materialize.ts`
(`veilCut.ts`, `veilWorker.ts`). Cut the art finer than Delve does, about
9 x 9 seeds, so a single patch is small.
- **A burn-in** is a cluster of 4 neighbouring patches around a random,
  not yet burned patch (2 under "half as much").
- Flares pick their spot at random; the player doesn't choose.
- The answer reveals the rest of the art with the usual finale.

### Zones

| Zone type | Free clue | Rule | Loot deal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | One common mod line | None | 3 cards |
| Incursion | Flavor text, name words masked | Flares burn in half as much | 3 cards |
| Ritual | The answer's word lengths (words and syllables per word) | Scrolls of Wisdom don't work | 3 cards |
| Blight | A small burn-in (2 patches) | 2 extra names | 3 cards |

If an item has no common mod line, the Plain clue falls back to its word
lengths.

### Life and the flask

As in v1: 100 life, never more. A miss costs a random 20 to 30 in zones 1 and
2, 30 to 40 in zones 3 and 4, 55 to 65 at the boss; the item shows the range
before submitting. Drinking a flask charge heals 10 at once and 10 after
each of the next 2 items; drinking again restarts it (the rest of the old
one is lost). Drink during an item only, at any moment, from the pack
like a supply. The town heals 20. Nothing else refills the flask except a Flask charge loot card.

### Supplies

| Supply | When used | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flare | Burns in a small part of the art at a random spot | 5 |
| Scroll of Wisdom | Shows one common mod line not yet shown, name words masked | 5 |
| Name strike | Strikes 1 wrong name, never below 3 names | 6 |
| Word lengths | Shows how many words the answer has and the syllables in each | 4 |
| First syllable | Places the answer's first syllable on the line, locked | 8 |
| Decoy sweep | Greys out 3 syllables that only wrong names use | 6 |

- Used up when used. A supply with nothing to do (no common line left, no
  decoy-only syllable left, word lengths already shown) is shown unusable.
- Silent boss: only Flares work.
- Word lengths shows a pattern such as "2 · 1 · 1 · 2" next to the line.

### Loot

A right answer deals loot cards face up, in the deal look of the other modes,
and the player takes one.
- **Cards dealt:** the zone's deal (3), minus 1 per miss on this item, never
  below 1, plus the item's corruption bonus.
- **One card is unidentified:** face down until taken. It rolls the
  uncommon and rare cards twice as often.

| Card | Gives | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | 4 to 8 in zones 1 and 2, 6 to 10 in zones 3 and 4 | 10 |
| Supply | A random supply from the table | 8 |
| Vaal Orb | Goes in the pack (takes a slot) | 3 |
| Flask charge | Refills one empty charge (rolled again if none is empty) | 2 |
| Jackpot | 25 gold (stands in for the boon card of the full design) | 1 |

A supply that doesn't fit in a full pack can replace one, or be left.

### Corruption

A Vaal Orb costs 4 at the vendor, drops as loot and takes a pack slot. It is
used on the item being answered, like a supply, and it corrupts the item: no
more supplies can be used on it (a flask charge still can). A card flips and
shows the outcome:

| Outcome | Weight | Effect on this item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gilded | 2 | +2 cards on its deal |
| Unveiled | 2 | A large burn-in at once (12 patches), +1 card |
| Twisted | 2 | 3 more names on the board (never above 10), +2 cards |
| Bricked | 1 | A right answer deals nothing |

The outcome holds through all the item's submits. Vaal Orbs can't be used on
boss items (the Silent rule already blocks them).

## Data (generated, with tests)

### `src/data/syllables.json` from `scripts/make-syllables.mjs`

1. "of", "the" and "and" stay whole; a possessive keeps "'s" on its last
   syllable.
2. No other word is a single tile. Compounds split at word boundaries and
   then by syllable; a one-syllable word splits before its vowel
   ("gr / ip", "b / ond").
3. The rest is hyphenated with Liang's patterns (npm package).
4. All lower case. Keep each name's word boundaries in the file (for auto
   spacing and Word lengths).

Tests: every name rebuilds from its syllables and word boundaries; no
syllable is empty; no tile is a whole word except the three small words.

### `src/data/clues.json` from `scripts/make-clues.mjs`

Per item: base, mod lines, flavor text, and for gems the support
description and Category line, name words masked as in v1 (fixed-width
`▮▮▮`). New:
- Each mod line gets a `shared` count: how many items have the same line
  once numbers are replaced (strip parentheses, any number or range becomes
  `#`). A line is **common** at 3 or more.
- Drop requirements; they are no longer used.

Measured on today's data: 399 of 501 items have at least one common line,
with a median of 2 per item.

Tests: no clue contains a masked word of its own item's name; `shared` is at
least 1 everywhere.

## Run log (updated)

Per item: `itemId`, `category`, `zone`, `zoneType`, `corruption` (or null),
`boss`, `names`, `board` (the names, the answer marked), `freeClue`,
`supplies` (type, order, ms), `burnIns`, `submits` (built text, right or
wrong, ms), `lifeBefore`, `lifeAfter`, `deal` (the cards offered, which was
unidentified), `took`, `drank` (at which moment of the item), `vaal` (outcome and how many supplies were used before it).

Per run: as v1, plus Vaal Orbs bought, found and used, corruption outcomes,
unidentified cards taken out of offered, and spacing on or off.

per Flare, seeds per side); loot deal size; loot card weights and gold
