# Wraeclast run v5: options (design session)

Answers `design-v5-handoff.md`. Options only; boards follow the owner's pick.

## What the two playtests say underneath

- **Information is too cheap.** 12 and then 18 supplies were left unused, and
  gold still ran out. Every item can buy its way to the answer, so knowing
  the item and not knowing it end the same way: a right answer, a few
  supplies down. The doc's boons combine around information, which would
  make that worse.
- **Life never matters later.** Life only matters when it hits 0, and the
  boss doesn't push it there. A toll at step 5 costs nothing if the towns
  heal it back before the boss.
- **The boss is just more items.** Rules that tax supplies (Murky,
  Lingering) don't bite when the pack is full.

So the recommendation below moves the axis from **cheaper clues** to
**answering with less**. Greed then lives in every item ("do I spend a
Torch, or answer bare for the bonus?") as well as in the route. And life
carries into a boss that hits back.

## 1. Growth

All three directions are dealt the same way, as already decided: answering
both items of a corrupted zone deals **1 of 3 picks**. The pick lasts the
run, and no vendor sells it. A run passes 3 to 5 corrupted zones, so it ends
with **2 to 4 picks**. That is few enough for each one to matter.

| | A. Lean picks (recommended) | B. Clue picks (the doc's boons, slimmed) | C. Pacts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What a pick does | Rewards an answer that used little: an item answered with no supply drops +1 card; a right first try heals 5; each unused supply at the boss deals 1 more damage (section 3) | Makes one clue stronger or one zone type richer: Torches burn in more; Scrolls show 2 lines; +1 card in Incursion zones | A strong edge with a lasting price: Torches burn in twice as much, but misses cost 10 more; +1 card on every drop, but each miss removes 2 |
| Do they combine | Yes, into an expert build that answers bare and gets paid for it | Yes, around clue types and zone types (route toward Incursion with a Scroll pick) | Weakly; the prices stack, which is the fun and the risk |
| Effect on the oversupply | Fixes it: unused supplies become worth something | Worsens it unless supplies get scarcer | Neutral |
| Greed | In every item and in the route | Mostly in the route | In the pick itself |
| Risk | A newcomer gets less from them (harsh is fine, principle 11) | Flattens further; a build that buys answers | Prices that never come due when nobody misses (playtest 5) |

**On the map:** a corrupted node already shows Blood toll and its front's
boss rule. It adds the pick's kind as one word under them, for example "Pick:
Lean" (the kinds are Lean and Clue, and Pact if C joins the pool). That names the
kind, not the three offers, so the route choice is "do I want this kind
enough to pay the toll". The three picks are dealt face up after the zone's
second item, in the card look.

**At 375 px:** picks sit as small seals in the in-game header, to the right
of life and gold, at most 4 (16 px each, Cinzel numerals not needed). Tapping
one opens a sheet listing all picks, one line each. A fifth pick replaces one
(the sheet asks which), so the cap is a choice too.

**Co-op:** picks are the party's, since the board is shared; the party picks
together the way it builds the name, and the host settles a tie. A Lean pick
counts the shared pack, so co-op stays one build, not one per player.

**Recommendation: A**, with one or two B picks kept in the pool for clue
types nobody buys (Base reveal, Word lengths), so the pool also nudges the
shop. Drop pure safety picks (Fortify, Second Wind): with v4d's stakes they
would be the always-right pick.

## 2. Items that differ

| | A. Zone types that change the play (recommended) | B. A Well that costs an item | C. Events between zones |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What | 3 or 4 zone types get a twist that changes how you answer, not only what you see (table below) | A Well holds one item with no supplies allowed. Right: the flask refills and 10 life. Wrong: a normal miss, no refill | The doc's Gambler and Strongbox as rare nodes: one bare item for a stake |
| Knowledge decides | Yes, each twist rewards knowing items | Yes, and a full flask no longer makes it a free skip | Yes (both are trivia) |
| Cost | Rules and wording for 4 types; the free clues stay | One rule; fixes playtest 5's free skip | 2 node types, more map |
| Risk | Too many rules for a newcomer; keep it to 4 | None worth naming | A board game between zones, which the doc warned about |

Twists for A, each one line at 375 px:

| Zone type | Twist (as it reads on the map and the item) |
| --- | --- |
| Ultimatum | "Before each item: take a rule for +1 card, or not" (greed by choice, item by item) |
| Ritual | "Pay 10 life for a clue instead of a supply" (life as a currency, a real trade under v4d) |
| Expedition | "Two items, one board: name both" (one Inscription holds two answers; knowing one clears the other's tiles) |
| Essence | "Name it from the base alone for +2 cards" (the free clue is the base; supplies still allowed, but the bonus goes) |

Incursion, Blight and Plain keep today's small rules, so a route still has
calm zones.

**Recommendation: A with B.** B is one rule and closes a hole the owner
already hit. C waits until A has been played.

## 3. The boss

| | A. A duel (recommended) | B. The run's exam | C. A rolled boss, from the start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How it works | The boss has life. Each right answer hits it: harder the fewer supplies you used (10 bare, 6 with one supply, 3 with more). Each item it hits you for 10 as it opens (v4d's Bloodied, made the boss's own). Items keep coming until one side is at 0 | The boss's items are the run's own: the ones you spent the most supplies on, now with no free clue | The doc's version: a boss rule shown on the map from step 1 (Blind, Mirrored, Hoarder, Shifting), and the shop and route prepare for it |
| Climax | Yes: life entering the boss is the fight's length, and the flask's one drink decides it | Medium: a test of what you learned this run | Low: playtests showed rules taxing a full pack don't bite |
| Preparation | All run long: life kept, supplies left (Lean picks), picks taken | Pay attention; lean answers mean easier boss items | Buy for the rule |
| Corruption | Each front still alive adds life to the boss (about one more item) and 5 to its hit | Each front alive adds an item | Each front alive adds a rule (today) |
| Growth | Lean picks add damage; unused supplies count | Little | Clue picks counter the rule |
| Risk | Tuning the numbers; an expert may end it in 3 items, which is fine | It can feel like a rerun; knowing the name you just built makes it easy | What playtests 4 and 5 already showed |

**Recommendation: A**, keeping C's rule as the boss's look and one twist
(Mirrored or Shifting), shown from step 1, so a boss still has a face. It
answers "how do I prepare from the start": life and spare supplies are the
preparation, so a toll at step 5 now costs something at step 10.

## 4. Choices that bite (with the recommendations)

| Choice | Today: always right | With the recommendations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corrupted or not | Playtest 4: never, the loot wasn't needed. Playtest 5: always, the toll was healed back | Toll life is boss life now, and the pick is worth it; stopping a front shrinks the boss. Each side has weight, so it's decided by life in hand and the pick's kind |
| Zone type | Pick the clue you know best | Same, plus the twists: Ultimatum and Essence pay a confident player; Ritual is a lifeline that costs boss life |
| Well | Always, with a full flask: a free skip | One bare item to earn the refill; a weak player avoids it |
| The shop | Buy Torches; gold runs out, supplies don't | With Lean picks and the duel, every bought supply you don't need is gold that did nothing, and every used one is less damage. Still easy if prices stay low: consider stacks of 2, not 3 |
| The flask | Never drunk | One drink, and the duel hits every item: when to drink is the boss's real question |
| The growth pick | (none yet) | Always right if a safety pick is offered, so the pool has none |

Still easy after all this: **the shop**, if supplies stay cheap. It is a
number to tune in playtest 6, not a rule.

## Names

The recommendation both ways. Item names are Path of Exile items you socket
for a lasting effect, which is what a pick does; none clashes with an answer
or a mode (checked against `items.json` and `src/`).

| Pick (A unless marked) | Item name | Plain name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where picks go | Soul Cores, socketed for the run (Vaal; dealt by corrupted zones, as the Vaal ruins drop them) | Picks, or marks |
| Bare answer: +1 card | Soul Core of Atmohua | Clean Hands |
| Right first try: heals 5 | Soul Core of Jiquani | Steady Hand |
| Unused supply: +1 boss damage | Soul Core of Xopec | Frugal |
| Ultimatum rule pays +2 cards | Soul Core of Zalatl | Bold Bet |
| (B) Base reveal also strikes 4 decoys | Soul Core of Citaqualotl | Appraiser |
| (B) Word lengths also places the first tile | Soul Core of Opiloti | Wordsmith |

- Soul Cores' game effects are stat bonuses, so "doing what it does" holds
  only loosely (it grants a lasting bonus once socketed); a research pass
  should match each pick to a core whose stat it echoes, as
  `research/supply-items.md` did for supplies.
- Avoid Tacati and Hayoxi: Tacati's Ire and Hayoxi's Fulmination are
  answers.
- The trade-off: "Soul Core of Atmohua" is 22 characters, and 4 of them
  don't say what they do. Plain names read at a glance at 375 px. If item
  names are chosen, the seal shows the effect's icon and the sheet carries
  the name.
- The boss needs no item name; it keeps the doc's generic titles.
