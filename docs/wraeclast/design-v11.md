# Wraeclast run: design v11, fixing v5

## The six root issues
- **"it's fiddly and cumbersome to find the right inscriptions even if you know the name"**: fixed. The syllable board goes. You type the name and press Enter, with the tell pinned above the keyboard. There's no list, so a guesser has nothing to pick from.
- **"there is no journey or exploration on the map"**: partly fixed. Each act is a web of real places whose paths fork, rejoin and dead-end. Fog hides everything past the zones next to you, and levels climb toward the wall. A place's name still picks no item, because PoE2 ties few uniques to a place.
- **"difficulty is a joke"**: fixed in the rules, but the numbers need play. You name from memory, once, with no list. Walls can't be skipped and show no art. You start with no supplies and earn them only by naming bare. Only towns heal.
- **"corruption is meaningless"**: fixed. The red blinds zones instead of adding numbers. Every second clear it spreads one ring from the wall. The next ring is outlined with a count, so you watch the window close.
- **"systems don't mesh well together"**: fixed. There's one loop, and all of it is knowing items: a tell, a name, a supply, sight on a blind zone, a bigger haul, paid for in life. Gold, the shop, boons, depth and mods are cut.
- **"the items feel uncreative and not exciting"**: partly fixed. Each zone shows its unique by something only it has: its art, its lore, or a line no other unique has. A right name opens the full tooltip and adds the unique to your haul, and the best uniques guard the walls. Every unique still plays the same way, though: a tell, then a name.

## Rules
1. There are three acts, each a web of real places. Act 1 runs from Clearfell Encampment to Temple of Kopec (11 zones), act 2 from Ziggurat Encampment to Heart of the Tribe (7), and act 3 from The Forest Encampment to the Seed on The Cathedral Rooftop (8).
2. You start at Clearfell with 100 life, the most you can have, and no supplies. You can enter any zone, or the wall, that links to a place you've cleared (towns count). Skip what you like; you never walk back.
3. A zone holds one unique from its act's levels (1 to 15, 16 to 35, 36 to 64; no lineage gems). The nearer the wall, the higher the level.
4. A zone that links to a cleared place shows one tell: a patch of its art, its lore, or a line no other unique has. A tell never holds part of the name. The rest is fog.
5. Type the name; Enter submits. Case, accents, spaces, punctuation and a leading "The" don't count. One wrong letter (two in names of 8 letters or more) is forgiven when no other unique is that close. Anything else reads "no such unique", and you retype for free.
6. Before typing you may spend supplies. A Torch shows more art, a Golden Page the lore, a Scroll of Wisdom the next mod line (rarest first), and an Ancient Orb the base type.
7. Right: the unique joins your haul. If you spent no supply in that zone, you also take a supply of your choice. Wrong: you lose 10, 15 or 20 life, by act. Either way its tooltip opens and the zone is cleared.
8. Every second clear, the red spreads one ring out from the wall. Unnamed zones it reaches go blind: no tell, only what your supplies show.
9. A wall deals 3, then 4, then 5 uniques from the top of its act's levels (the Seed's from level 65 and up), one at a time. Each shows its line or its lore, never art. You get one name each. A miss costs 20, then 25, then 30, and walls pay no supplies.
10. Past a wall, the next town heals 30 and the red goes back to its wall.
11. Break the Seed to win; your haul is your score. At 0 life the run ends.

## A run
- **Act 1.** 100 life, empty pack. Freythorn shows "Reflects opposite Ring", which is Kalandra's Touch: you keep an Ancient Orb.
- Ogham Manor's lore, "It is safer to be feared than to be loved", is Bramblejack: a Golden Page. The red takes The Library, Buried Shrines and Pondium.
- Kaom's Stronghold shows a gold cap: Goldrim, and a Torch. The Library, your way in, is blind. The Orb shows Wrapped Sandals, so it's Wanderlust.
- Temple of Kopec deals three, none by art. Doomgate goes bare, the Torch finds Ezomyte Peak, and Birthright Buckle beats you and your Page: 80 life. Ziggurat Encampment heals you to 100.
- **Act 2.** Bones of Ullr, Carrion Call and Original Sin pay a Scroll, a Torch and a Page. A detour to Holten costs 15 on a mask you can't place (Leer Cast).
- Journey's End is blind. The Page shows the lore of a Brinerot crew's idle favourite: The Black Cat. Heart of the Tribe deals four. You name three, and Heartbound Loop beats you: 60. The Forest Encampment heals you to 90.
- **Act 3.** Headhunter, Blood of the Warrior and Lightning Coil pay three supplies. Sandstorm Visage beats you: 70. Both ways to the wall are blind, so you burn a Torch at Tidal Island rather than take 20, and find Mageblood.
- The Cathedral Rooftop deals five. You name Kaom's Heart, Mjölner and Atziri's Splendour; Thunderfist and Morior Invictus beat you. The Seed breaks with you on 10 life: a haul of 20 from 26 names, in about 13 minutes.

## What v5 loses
- The syllable board: tiles, decoy names, the look-alike walk, marks, Clear and Identify, tap-only entry, and retrying until right.
- Gold, the shop and its prices, the boon draft and all 14 boons.
- Depth, waystone mods, corrupted zones' numbers, and the duel's numbers (the Seed's life and hit, your hits of 20, 12 and 6).
- The lair open from the start, the frontier with no fog, the 14-place region and the lineage gems.
- Kept: real places and links, one unique per zone, the three towns, the four supplies, the Seed on The Cathedral Rooftop, the Veil, the red veins, the run log and the tuning panel.

## Questions
1. When a unique beats you, should you meet it again?
   - Gone for good; its tooltip has already shown you what it is (recommended)
   - It comes back at the Seed's wall, so a shaky run ends harder
2. How fast should the red close in?
   - Every second clear (recommended)
   - Every clear: tighter, with more blind zones, so supplies matter more
   - Every third clear: gentler, with more of the map in sight
