## The stories

**Rushing the lair won.** Run 2 went from the camp straight to the lair with
no zone, no boon and no supply. The Seed at its starting 30 life died to two
bare answers. Between them came five misses in a row (Bitterbloom, Prayers
for Rain, The Surrender, Trephina, Widowhail, each a decoy that shares the
real name's first letters), at 15 life each (10 for the miss, 5 for the
Seed's hit), and the run still ended at 15 life. The map, the zones, the
spread, the gold and the draft never came into it.

**Towns chained without zones.** Run 1 cleared one zone, then entered two
towns back to back (Ziggurat Encampment, then The Forest Encampment) and
drafted at each, so it held three boons after one zone. The Dagger made a
Torch answer hit 24, and the Seed at 38 life died in two answers. The build
had no time to work or fail: two duel items, one boon firing. The spread
reached 1.

**Slow on a phone.** Duel boards had 32 to 36 tiles and 4 to 5 decoy names;
an item took about a minute (Trephina 172 s, then missed). The first draft
screen took 52 s. In run 1 more than half the run was drafts, shops and the
map, not items.

Blackflame came up in both duels.
