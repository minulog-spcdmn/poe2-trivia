# Wraeclast run: design v7, the town

## 1. Pay the price by name

- **A visit:** each affliction you carry names the stat that pays it off ("Weakened Flesh • pay off with Life"). The town lays out 3 uniques of one kind by name only, with no art and no mods. Pay gold and tap the one that has it.
- A right tap clears the affliction and keeps its boon. A wrong one keeps the price, and the cards turn over so you learn them. One try per affliction per town.
- **The hook:** someone who knows the names can clear prices a stranger can't, so they take more of the Pledges the corrupted zones deal. Boons pile up until the Seed melts, and the only limit is the gold you don't spend on supplies.
- **The trivia:** it runs name to mods, the reverse of a zone. A PoE player sees Drillneck among three quivers and knows it rolls Life; a stranger guesses 1 in 3.
- **The data:** one of the 3 carries the word in its real mod lines (never base stats) and two of the same group don't, so the kind gives nothing away and a script picks them.

## 2. The false line

- **A visit:** the town shows one real item in full: art, name, base, mod lines and lore. One whole line came from a near twin, another unique on the same base or in the same group.
- Tap the line you think is false. A right catch pays gold, a wrong tap costs gold, and passing is free.
- **The hook:** every fake is close by design. A miss shows the real line and the item it came from, and you think "I knew that". Next visit you catch subtler swaps.
- **The trivia:** only someone who knows the item sees which line doesn't belong. Whole lines only, never a number, and the town's item never comes up in that run's zones, so it spoils nothing.
- **The data:** 48 bases carry 2 or more uniques, so twins are plenty and a script makes every swap.

## 3. Your stash is your build

- **A visit:** pick 3 of the uniques you named this run to wear, any kind. You see each by name and art only, never its mods, and you can't wear them all.
- **In the duel:** a mod word on two worn pieces (Cold, Evasion, Minions) raises your hit, and on all three it raises it more. Nothing else about them counts.
- **The hook:** every zone might hold the piece that completes your set, and a full set snowballs into the duel.
- **The trivia:** only knowing what Ab Aeterno and the rest really do tells you which three belong together. Show the mods and it's a sorting puzzle anyone solves.

## The pick

**Pay the price by name.** It plugs knowing items straight into the Pledges you already have, with no new system, and it's one tap on a phone: three names, pick one.
Knowing what a name does is the whole edge, so an expert walks into the lair with boons a stranger couldn't afford, and every miss turns the cards over to teach you.

## Questions

1. How far should an expert be able to break a run with it?
   - **Wide open (recommended):** gold is the only limit, so someone who knows the names clears most prices.
   - **Capped:** each payoff costs more gold than the last, so even an expert clears one or two a run.
   - **Rare:** one payoff a town at most.

2. How should the town show the 3 uniques?
   - **Name only (recommended):** it asks what a name does, the reverse of a zone.
   - **Name and art:** kinder to players who know items by sight.
