# Wraeclast run: design v9, a new loop

## What playtest 6 showed

- The owner played v5 twice on a phone and was "not impressed".
- It was easy to rush the boss, the boons felt flat, an item took about a minute on a 32 to 36 tile board, and nothing pulled toward another run.
- No moment was fun, naming an item included, so the loop and the item screen both start over.

## Identify

- **Moment.** Each foe is an unidentified unique. Its real tooltip fills in a line every 1.5 s, most shared text first, art last. Tap its name from 4 the moment you know it; the more lines still hidden, the harder you hit. The 3 decoys are real look-alikes of the same class, so no early line gives it away.
- **Run.** Four fights of 3 items, then the Seed of Corruption: 5 items, faster lines. 10 HP for the run; a wrong tap, or letting the art show untapped, costs 3. Between fights, pick one line to show first in the next fight: the base, a mod or the lore. A blind tap is a 1 in 4 guess that costs HP, so rushing loses. About 4 minutes.
- **Play again.** The codex keeps the earliest line you named each item at, and your furthest fight. A win unlocks a faster line clock.
- **Keeps.** The tooltip (ItemDetails, in Fontin), real look-alike names, the Seed. Drops the board, supplies, boons, map and towns.

## Pick Your Fights

- **Moment.** Three item arts face up in the card look, no names. Tap the one you trust, then name it from 6 real look-alikes on Delve's 16 s clock. About 10 s a pick.
- **Run.** 4 acts of 6 steps, 3 lives; a wrong name or a timeout costs one. The two arts you pass join the Seed's pile, face up beside the steps, so you watch it grow. Each act ends with the Seed throwing back the 4 from that pile you know worst by your codex, at 8 names, mirrored and burned in. No map, nothing to skip: a dodged art comes back harder. About 7 minutes.
- **Play again.** A loss names the item and where you passed it ("Ab Aeterno, act 2 step 3"). Then climb Waystone tiers, one added rule each, and race a shared daily seed.
- **Keeps.** The Seed as each act's boss, the card look, real look-alike names. Everything else goes.

## Swarm

- **Moment.** Three lanes, each with a monster creeping down over about 8 s, holding one item's art. Tap a monster, then its item's name from 4 real look-alikes: about 3 s. The choice, over and over, is which art you know well enough to take first.
- **Run.** 10 waves, one per real region, about 50 s each; the last is the Seed, faster, with 8 names. After each wave, hang 1 of 3 items you killed on your belt (3 slots). From then on, monsters carrying that kind of item (rings, helmets and so on, 28 kinds) show 2 names, not 4. 5 lives; a monster that gets through or a wrong name costs one. Waves keep their own clock, so there is nothing to rush. About 9 minutes.
- **Play again.** The end screen shows your best wave and the item that got through. The codex fills with every item you killed. Each win starts the next swarm one step faster, and you choose when to climb.
- **Keeps.** The Seed as the last wave, real look-alike names, real regions as backdrops, Delve's one-tap names and clock. Drops the board, gold, supplies, towns, boons and map.

## Pick: Identify

It is the only pitch that changes the moment of naming itself, which playtest 6 found no fun: every second is a bet to call now or wait one more line.

One tap, no board or shop, and a blind rush costs HP, so it answers the slow item and the easy rush at once.

## Questions

1. Which feel should the next prototype chase?
   - **Identify (recommended):** tense seconds on every item; bases, mods and lore count, art comes last.
   - **Pick Your Fights:** a calmer quiz; every item you dodge comes back at the Seed.
   - **Swarm:** the most Hades-like rush; small art moving on a phone.
2. Identify's only build is the line you choose to show first. Is that enough?
   - **Yes, the call itself is the hook (recommended):** keep it bare and see if the bet on each line pulls you back.
   - **No, a run needs something that grows:** picks should stack into something you chase, like Hades.
3. In Identify, should a player who knows only the art be able to win?
   - **Yes, slowly and narrowly (recommended):** waiting for the art is a safe but thin path to the Seed.
   - **No:** the win is for players who know mods and lore; art alone deals too little.
