# Wraeclast run: design v8, the town (the owner's sketch)

> "getting a random selection of boons you can choose from a Limited number and they combine and become a build that can work or fail like in hades"

## A town visit
1. Tap a town on the map. Three boon cards rise in the usual card look, each a name and one line ("Ornate Dagger • Torch only: hits the Seed double").
2. Tap one to take it, or pass. The other two are gone. It joins your seals in the header, 4 at most; to take a fifth, tap the seal you give up.
3. From the second town on, one of the three always names the same supply as a boon you hold (or no supply, for a bare build).
4. Then the shop, as now: Torch, Golden Page, Ancient Orb and Scroll of Wisdom, bought with gold.
5. Tap Leave to go back to the map. One decision per screen.

## How boons become a build that works or fails
- Each boon names one supply, or no supply. Most fire only when you name an item using that supply alone; a few make that supply cheaper or more plentiful. Boons on the same supply fire together. Boons on different supplies split you, like scattered gods in Hades.
- A run that breaks: Ornate Dagger at town 1, Balbala's Gift at town 2, Silver Tongue at town 3. Torches come free or at half price, and every Torch-only answer hits the Seed for 24, double a clued hit.
- An art expert goes into the lair after 5 zones and kills a 70-life Seed in three answers.
- The same seals fail in Forgotten zones where the art means nothing to you. You miss, or you buy a Scroll to be safe, and that item fires nothing.
- Spread your picks over four supplies and each boon fires rarely. You meet the Seed bare.

## Why it's about knowing items
- The build is a bet on how you know items: by sight (Torch), lore (Golden Page), kind (Ancient Orb), stats (Scroll of Wisdom), or cold from the tiles alone. Your picks are the guess, and the run shows if it was right.
- A lore player who takes art boons misses on Torch-only answers, and the Pages they fall back on fire nothing. Ancient Orb and no-supply builds are the strongest, because only an expert names an item from its kind or from bare tiles.
- Take the trivia away and no boon ever fires.

## Starter boons
- Ornate Dagger: an item you name with only the Torch hits the Seed double.
- Balbala's Gift: your first Torch at each town is free.
- Honoured Challenger: an item you name with only the Golden Page heals 15 life.
- Raincaller: an item you name with only the Golden Page pays double gold.
- Sanguine Vial: each item you name with only the Ancient Orb takes 5 off the Seed's life.
- Hare Foot: a zone whose item you name with only the Ancient Orb doesn't advance the spread.
- Fright Mask: each item you name with only the Scroll of Wisdom makes the Seed hit 1 less for the rest of the run, never below 1.
- Sacred Mirror: every Scroll of Wisdom you buy comes as two.
- Assassin's Blade: an item you name with no supply hits the Seed double.
- Upward Path: each item in a row you name with no supply pays 5 more gold than the last; buying a supply ends the streak.
- Silver Tongue: the supplies your boons name cost half.

## What it replaces
- Boons move from corrupted zones to towns: 3 offered, take 1 or pass, hold 4 at most. They carry no affliction, so Pledges and their prices go. A boon's price is the two cards you leave, the cap of 4, and answering from one clue.
- Corrupted zones keep only the deeper depth and the waystone mod. The supplies, the shop and the duel stay as settled.

## Questions
1. How should a town pick its three cards?
   - **One fits your build (recommended):** from the second town on, one card names a supply you already hold, so a build forms in a few picks. You never see the rule.
   - **Fully random:** truest to "random", but a build often never shows up, so luck can decide the run.
   - **A mark on each town:** each town shows one supply on the map, like a god's face on a Hades door, so you route toward your build. It adds icons and mixes routing with boon picks.
2. Should any boon need two supplies?
   - **No, one supply per build (recommended):** builds stack one clue, and variety comes from the random cards.
   - **A few Duo boons:** as in Hades, a Duo is offered only once you hold boons of two supplies, so the strongest builds mix clues and the next run has something new to chase.
