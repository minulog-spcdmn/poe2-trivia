# Wraeclast run: design brief v7 (a fresh start on the town)

For a fresh design session. Read this, then `STATE.md`. Read
`design-v6-options.md` (branch `claude/design-4ca3ba`) only for sections
1, 2 and 6 if you need the map, zones or duel; skip sections 3 to 11.

## The plot

poe2.quest is item trivia for Path of Exile players. Wraeclast run is its
roguelike mode. **Every system has to be about knowing items:** their names,
art, lore, bases, mods and where they drop. If a mechanic would work the same
with the trivia removed, it's off the plot.

## Settled (owner, Oct 19)

- An open region of about 14 real Wraeclast places; enter any place next to
  one you've cleared. A zone is 1 item. Each clear spreads the Seed of
  Corruption one ring: a soft timer. The lair is open from the start; you go
  when you think you'll win the duel.
- Zones: depth (Known, Obscure, Forgotten) plus at most one waystone mod.
- The boss is the Seed of Corruption, fought as a duel.
- Growth is Pledges (PoE2's word), each a Trial of the Sekhemas boon with an
  affliction as its price.
- Four supplies, all bought with gold: Torch (the art), Golden Page (lore),
  Ancient Orb (the kind of item), Scroll of Wisdom (stats).
- The item screen (Inscription) and real look-alike decoy names stay.
- About 10 minutes a run.

## Rejected, and why

- Town gambles borrowed from other games (rolled market and rerolls,
  Stacked Decks, haggling, mystery boxes, slots, auctions): "so
  uncreative... straight up ripped from other games and overused".
- Six original town gambles (lucky syllables, scouting a clue, trophies,
  doomed towns, a shrine item, a bounty item): none "sound like something I
  would want to play".
- Crafting a weapon with orbs, linking skill and support gems, upgrading
  supplies: "strays way too far from what poe2.quest is... you have lost the
  plot". Supplies costing life, reward or the Seed's life: "why make it this
  complicated?"

## The ask

The town should be "something that hooks players and makes them play round
after round". The owner loves all four of these hooks: breaking the game (a
combo that snowballs), unlocks between runs, build synergies, near-miss
mastery. Find how those hooks come from **knowing items**, at a town, simply.
Simple beats clever: the owner rejected complication more than once.

Write 3 short pitches (not variants), each in 5 lines or fewer: what a town
visit is, why it hooks, how it stays item trivia. Then your pick and at most
2 multiple-choice questions. Text only, no boards, nothing built. Put it in
`docs/wraeclast/design-v7-town.md` on your branch, push, and reply to the
orchestrator in under 8 lines.
