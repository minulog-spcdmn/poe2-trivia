# Wraeclast run: naming research (supplies and boss rules)

Rules applied: every name is from PoE1/PoE2, and a reused item, service or mechanic must do here what it does in the game. "Stretch" means the theme fits but the in-game function only partly matches.

Note on sources: poewiki.net, poe2wiki.net and pathofexile.fandom.com all returned a bot-protection "Access Denied" page, so nothing below comes from them. Facts come from poe2db.tw / poedb.tw (data mined item text, NPC and boss dialogue) and from poe-vault.com, maxroll.gg and gamerguides.com guides. Only pages I actually opened are listed.

---

## A. Supplies

### A1. "Requirement reveal" (level and attribute requirements)

**Nothing in PoE does this.** In the game, level and attribute requirements are never hidden: you can see them even on unidentified items. So no PoE consumable "reveals requirements", and anything you use here is a stretch. The closest honest option is a smith NPC, someone who judges what a piece of gear demands of the person carrying it.

| Option | Why it fits | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Renly** (PoE2, blacksmith of Clearfell Encampment, Act 1) • **top pick** | He is the Ezomyte smith whose forge and tools are the campaign's gear service ("Forgework tools belong to Renly and his son"). A smith sizing up a weapon's weight and demands is the natural reader of requirements. It also matches the existing boon name "Smith's Eye". | Yes. In the game he forges and unlocks the salvage bench. He doesn't appraise. | https://poe2db.tw/us/Renly |
| **Tarkleigh** (PoE1, Lioneye's Watch, Act 1 weapon and armour vendor) | He is the first gear vendor in PoE1, the old soldier who outfits exiles. | Bigger. He only sells gear. | https://poedb.tw/us/Tarkleigh |

Suggestion: name the supply after the NPC, for example "Renly's" plus what he gives, and say plainly in the design doc that it's an NPC service, not a game item.

### A2. "Lore reveal" (flavor text, name words masked)

| Option | Why it fits | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Golden Page** (PoE1 quest item, Act 3 "A Fixture of Fate") • **top pick** | A lost page of Vaal text. You bring it to Siosa, the "sole scholar" of the Imperius Intellection, and he reads it back as lore: "my translation skills are not what Icius' were. I can achieve a meagre paraphrase, at best. But a little knowledge is better than none". That matches this supply: lore text with gaps (the masked name words). The item itself carries a flavor quote from Icius Perandus. | Small. In the game it's a quest turn-in, not a consumable, but what it does (turns a page into readable lore, partly translated) is the same job. | https://poedb.tw/us/Golden_Page ; https://poedb.tw/us/A_Fixture_of_Fate ; https://poedb.tw/us/Siosa |
| **Siosa** (PoE1 NPC, the Library in Sarn) | The archivist who keeps "this grand repository of knowledge" and translates lost texts. Use him if you'd rather name an NPC service. | Small, same as above. | https://poedb.tw/us/Siosa |
| **The Hooded One** (PoE2 NPC, Act 1 onward) | He identifies items for free ("Bring me any magical objects you find. I can identify them for you."), and he is the campaign's keeper of old knowledge. | Medium. Identifying is already the Scroll of Wisdom's job, so two supplies would claim the same thing. I also couldn't confirm that flavor text is hidden on unidentified uniques (the wiki was blocked). | https://poe2db.tw/us/The_Hooded_One ; https://www.gamerguides.com/path-of-exile-2/guide/getting-started/tips-and-tricks/identifying-magical-items-for-free |

Rejected:
- **Silver Coin.** It used to buy a cryptic prophecy from Navali, which would have fit, but today the item "Creates a portal to the Halls of the Dead... Trial of the Ancestors". Its current job doesn't match. (https://poedb.tw/us/Silver_Coin)
- **Chronicle of Atzoatl.** It opens the Incursion temple. It doesn't reveal text. (https://poedb.tw/us/Chronicle_of_Atzoatl)

### A3. "Name strike" (strike one wrong candidate off the board)

| Option | Why it fits | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interrogation** (PoE1 Betrayal, the Interrogate choice on the Immortal Syndicate board) • **top pick** | Betrayal is literally a board of named suspects. Interrogating a member "puts them in prison for the next three Syndicate encounters", which temporarily removes them from the Syndicate (the board), and you gain intelligence. Striking a name off a board of candidates is the same action. Bonus parallel: when a safehouse leader is interrogated, "they will be replaced by the next highest ranking member", which echoes the Shifting boss replacing struck names. | Small. In Betrayal the member isn't "wrong", just taken off the board, but "take one name off the board" is exactly what the supply does. | https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/immortal-syndicate-guide ; https://poedb.tw/us/Betrayal |
| **Jun Ortoi** (PoE1 NPC who runs the Syndicate investigation) | The NPC version of the same idea, if you'd rather name the supply after a person. | Small. | (same Betrayal sources) |

Rejected:
- **Defer** (Ritual). It doesn't eliminate anything: it "guarantees its spawn on a following Ritual map with a discounted price". (https://www.sportskeeda.com/mmo/path-exile-2-poe2-ritual-guide)
- **Dynamite** (Delve). It blasts an obstacle, which is the same "removes something, but not a wrong answer" problem as Orb of Annulment.

## B. Boss rules

Two candidates per rule. Bosses in mainland Wraeclast come first wherever one fits.

### Blind (the art can't be seen at all; text clues still work)

1. **Count Geonor** (PoE2 Act 1, Ogham Manor, Wraeclast) • **top pick**. In his second phase, "Freezing Fog" covers "most of the arena in a chilling fog", and he "periodically jump[s] at the player from the fog". The guide's advice is to "Listen to the lines of his poem; after each line finishes, he leaps at the player from the mist." You can't see him, but his words tell you what is coming. That is the rule exactly: the Veil stays down and only text clues help. Sources: https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-1-boss-guide ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Count_Geonor
2. **The King in the Mists** (PoE2 Act 1, Freythorn, Wraeclast; also the Ritual pinnacle). He lives in the mists his name refers to, and he teleports around his arena. Sources: https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-1-boss-guide ; https://poe2db.tw/us/The_King_in_the_Mists
- Trap to avoid: **The Blind Beast** is an Act 4 boss on the Isle of Kin, outside Wraeclast, and it is the beast that is blind, not the player. The name only fits on the surface. (https://poe2db.tw/us/The_Blind_Beast ; https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-4-boss-guide)

### Silent (only Flares work; no text clues)

No PoE2 boss in Wraeclast clearly has silence in its lore or fight. These two are the most honest options:
1. **The Maven** (PoE1, Atlas) • **top pick, for how the fight plays**. Her "Memory Game" is purely visual: one of 3 sections "flashes up in a Purple color. You have to repeat the correct order", and every wrong step makes things worse. Art only, no words, and you're punished for mistakes. Not in Wraeclast. Source: https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide
2. **The Arbiter of Ash** (PoE2 endgame, Burning Monolith). This one fits on lore: his lines include "Her only salvation... is written in ash." and "Ashes to Ashes.", so the written word burned to ash leaves only the image. It's a stretch, because nothing in his fight takes away information. Source: https://poe2db.tw/us/The_Arbiter_of_Ash

### Mirrored (every patch mirrored and grayscale)

1. **Atziri** (Vaal queen; PoE2 Atziri, the Red Queen, fought in the Vaal temple; Vaal lore is set in Wraeclast's jungle, Act 3) • **top pick**. Her lore centres on her mirror. "Winning a single glance away from her mirror meant more than their lives." (Atziri's Allure). "She gazes into a mirror still" (Delve intro). Alva on the Atziri fight: "I bought us time by destroying the mirror". Siosa adds that she "wished to see her likeness reflected in the still waters of history". The PoE2 fight also brings "Mirrors of Refraction" into the arena. Grayscale is my addition and isn't in her lore. Sources: https://poe2db.tw/us/Atziri ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Atziri%2C_the_Red_Queen ; https://poedb.tw/us/Siosa ; https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/who-is-atziri-and-how-do-you-beat-poe2s-new-pinnacle-boss
2. **Diamora, Song of Death** (PoE2 Act 4, Singing Caverns, outside mainland Wraeclast). She covers the arena with her gaze, and looking at her builds petrification. She raises "walls of water... that has Diamora's reflection in them. Looking at them also gives stacks of petrification." Reflections plus turning to grey stone covers both halves of the rule (mirrored and grayscale). Source: https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-4-boss-guide

### Hoarder (every supply used takes two from your pack)

1. **Kitava, the Insatiable** (Karui god of hunger; fought in PoE1 Acts 5 and 10; invoked in PoE2 Act 4 as "Kitava's Thirst") • **top pick**. Item lore: "Tukohama, Tawhoa and Kitava went to the lake to fish... Kitava swallowed the entire lake, fish and all, with a single gulp." and "he would devour every soul in Hinekora's domain". A god who takes double, or everything, is the rule exactly. Not mainland Wraeclast. Sources: https://poedb.tw/us/Kitava ; https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-4-boss-guide
2. **The Bloated Miller** (PoE2 Act 1, the Riverbank, Wraeclast). Weak: this fits on the name only. "Bloated" suggests gluttony, but his recorded lines are just "Saw you in half!" and there's no hoarding lore. Source: https://poe2db.tw/us/The_Bloated_Miller
- Weaker alternative: **Balbala** (PoE2 Act 2, Vastiri). The Djinn demands her coin as a toll ("My barya, my coin, will grant you access"). It's about a toll, not hoarding. Source: https://poe2db.tw/us/Balbala
- I found no PoE2 boss in Wraeclast whose lore is about hoarding.

### Shifting (after each miss the board reshuffles and struck names are replaced)

1. **Zarokh, the Temporal** (PoE2, Trial of the Sekhemas, which you reach in Act 2's Vastiri, Wraeclast) • **top pick**. He undoes progress. His "Temporal Rewind" "creates a clock that winds backwards before teleporting the player to the oldest mirage", and his lines are "Reverse the flow of time...", "Time flows in all directions.", "Time unwinds." After a miss, the board rewinds: struck names come back as new ones. Sources: https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/zarokh-the-temporal-boss-guide ; https://maxroll.gg/poe2/bosses/zarohk-the-temporal-boss-guide ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Zarokh%2C_the_Temporal
2. **Sirus, Awakener of Worlds** (PoE1, Atlas). He fights by reshuffling. He "teleports to a random location and spawn[s] 12 clones in a large circle, while hiding among them", and "splits into 4 illusions". The board keeps changing and you have to find the real one again. Not in Wraeclast. Source: https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide
- Also fits, outside Wraeclast: **Yama the White** (PoE2 Act 4). "Monkey Business: Yama disappears, and chaos clones appear on the pillars... You need to find the real Yama". Source: https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-4-boss-guide
