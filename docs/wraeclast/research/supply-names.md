# Wraeclast run: names for the six working-name supplies

Rule applied (principle 8): a PoE item, currency, NPC service or mechanic name is used only when it does here what it does in the game. Anything that doesn't fit stays a labelled working name. "Stretch" means the theme fits but the in-game function only partly matches.

This replaces the supply half of `names.md`, which was written for an older supply set. Its Golden Page pick for Lore still stands. Its Interrogation pick moves from the cut Name strike to the Decoy sweep.

Note on sources: the wikis block bots, so nothing below comes from them. Facts come from poe2db.tw and poedb.tw (data mined item text and NPC dialogue) and from poe-vault.com, maxroll.gg and a few guide sites. Every source listed is a page I opened. Where I couldn't confirm something, I say so.

## 2. Category reveal (the item's category, such as Rings)

**Nothing in PoE reveals an item's class**, because it's never hidden: an item on the ground already shows it. What PoE does have is labels that tell you what kind of loot waits before you take it. That's the closest honest match, and it's a stretch.

| Option | What it is in PoE | Why it fits | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blueprint Reveal** (Heist) • **top pick** | PoE1 Heist. "Reveals are used to unlock additional Wings and Rooms of the Blueprints." Reward side rooms are "locked behind the doors marked with a specific type of reward they contain". Gianna's perk: "Discovers 1 Blueprint Reveals upon completion of a Heist Contract Mission". The Blueprint item even names its prize's kind up front: "Heist Targets: Enchanted Armaments". | You spend a Reveal and learn what kind of loot a room holds before you take it. Here you spend one and learn what kind of item it is before you name it. | Medium. A Reveal opens a room, and the kind is written on its door; Heist's kinds are broad (Armour, Jewellery, Gems), not one category like Rings. Only PoE1 players who ran Heist will know the term. | https://poedb.tw/us/Blueprints ; https://maxroll.gg/poe/currency/heist-blueprints-farming-guide ; https://poedb.tw/us/Gianna%2C_the_Master_of_Disguise |
| **Strongbox names** (Jeweller's, Armourer's, Blacksmith's) | PoE1 and PoE2. A strongbox's name says what it holds: Jeweller's "Amulets, Belts, Jewels, Rings, Hubris"; Armourer's "Armour Items"; Blacksmith's "Weapon Items" (PoE1 guide). PoE2 has the same types; poe2db lists them but doesn't state contents. | The label tells you the kind of loot before you open it. | Medium, with two problems. There is no one name: the label would change with the category, and no prefix covers every category. And "Strongbox" is already this mode's event, so a strongbox-named supply would read as a box. Not recommended. | https://poe-vault.com/guides/guide-to-strongboxes-in-path-of-exile ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Strongbox |

Also checked: Nenet, the Scout (Heist's Perception rogue) only scouts patrols ("Can Scout nearby Enemy Patrols and Elite Patrols during Heists"), so she doesn't fit. (https://poedb.tw/us/Nenet%2C_the_Scout)

If Blueprint Reveal feels too far, keep the working name.

## 3. Base reveal (the base type; expensive, because it's expert trivia)

**Nothing in PoE reveals a base type either**, because it's always visible. I believe an unidentified unique shows its base and art but not its name, which is exactly this supply's clue; I couldn't confirm that, because the wikis block bots.

| Option | What it is in PoE | Why it fits | Stretch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gwennen** • **top pick** | The Expedition gambler, in PoE1 and PoE2. She "shows you a set of base types with unknown properties"; you buy one and "receive a random item of that base type back". "Sometimes, it can be a very valuable rare or unique item." poe2db keeps a "Gwennen Cheat Sheet" that lists uniques under each base heading. | She shows you a base and you bet on which unique it could be. That is this supply's trivia: you see the base and name the unique. Her cheat sheet is the expert knowledge the supply is priced for. | Small to medium. She sells bases; she doesn't show the base of an item you already hold. Two cautions: the owner rejected a "Gwennen stall" as a bolted-on gamble, so her name may bring that idea back. And the mode's Gambler event is the natural home for her name; using her here takes it from that event. | https://poedb.tw/us/Gwennen ; https://poe2db.tw/us/Gwennen |
| **Renly** | PoE2 NPC, Act 1, the Clearfell smith. He sells and buys weapons and armour: "I'll smith for you what I can". | A smith knows a base by sight. He's free now that the Requirement reveal is cut. | Large. He forges and sells; he doesn't appraise. | https://poe2db.tw/us/Renly |

Rejected:
- **Orb of Chance**: "Unpredictably either upgrades a Normal item to Unique rarity or destroys it". Chancing uses the same knowledge (which uniques a base can become), but the orb turns a base into a unique; it shows nothing. Reusing it breaks the rule. (https://poe2db.tw/us/Orb_of_Chance)
- **Omen of the Ancients**: its next Orb of Chance upgrades the item to a random unique of the same class. Same problem. (https://poe2db.tw/us/Omen)

## 7. The Flare clash

**What poe2.quest's Delve Flare does** (from `src/`):
- You win one from a Flare Cache, a find card ("Answer right for a flare"), and carry up to 3 (`DELVE_MAX_FLARES`).
- It burns by itself when the answering player's clock hits 0 and adds 6 seconds (`FLARE_MS = 6000`, `src/lib/delve.ts`). It never works on a find's own question.
- On screen, the pack tooltip reads "Flare: When your time runs out, it burns and gives you six more seconds. It doesn't work on finds." (`ITEM_TIPS`, `src/lib/difficultyText.ts`). The codex counts "Flares burnt" with "6 s more on the clock each", and the "fully-laden" achievement names flares.
- As it burns, it lights the dark behind the item box, and the dark "seeps back in from the edges as its seconds run out" (`src/lib/flareBurn.ts`).

**What a Flare does in PoE** (Delve): "Flares are portable light sources that can be thrown to specific locations and last for a short while." The mine's darkness "rapidly drains your life and energy shield" outside the Crawler's light. A flare buys a short stay in a dark spot. (https://poedb.tw/us/Delve ; https://www.ludo.guide/guide/path-of-exile-2/delve-the-azurite-mine)

**What the Wraeclast Flare does:** it burns in about a sixth of the veiled art at a random spot, for the rest of the item.

**Does it break the rule?** Not as I read it. Principle 8 asks whether a PoE name does what it does in PoE. Both flares are a PoE flare: a short light thrown into the dark. Delve's turns the light into time, because its dark is the clock running out. Wraeclast's turns it into seen art, because its dark is the veil over the picture. Of the two, Wraeclast's is the more literal: you light a spot and see what's there. The gaps are small: PoE's goes where you throw it and fades, while Wraeclast's lands at random and stays.

**What it does strain** is one site with one word for two effects. A Delve player might expect a Wraeclast Flare to add time. That risk is small: Wraeclast has no clock at all, the shop card says what it does, and the two never meet in one mode.

**Options:**
1. **Keep both** • recommended. Each matches PoE, and each tooltip says what it does. Optional: give the Wraeclast Flare Delve's red road-flare glyph, so it reads as the same object used on a different dark. Cost: nothing.
2. **Rename the Wraeclast Flare.** It costs the mode its most honest PoE name, and I found nothing else in PoE that lights part of a dark area. Delve's other light is the Crawler, a cart that carries its light with it, not a supply.
3. **Rename Delve's Flare.** Delve is live. Its Flare is in the tooltips, the Flare Cache find, the codex, an achievement, its glyph and its effects. And it's a Delve flare in a mode called Delve, the most authentic use of the name there is. Not worth it.

## 8. Name collision check

I matched every candidate as a whole word against each name, base, group and category in `src/data/items.json` (501 items) and each name in `src/data/fakes.json` (1,503 real and fake names). **No candidate is an item or a fake.** Near misses worth knowing:
- **Hinekora's Lock**: "Hinekora's Sight" is a real unique; "Hinekora's Glimpse" and "Hinekora's Veil" are fakes. One reason it's rejected.
- **Blueprint**: "Doryani's Blueprint" is a fake.
- **Fracturing Orb**: "Fractured Empyrean" is a fake.
- **Remnant**: "Remnant of Darkness" is a fake.
- **Omen of Sinistral Exaltation**: "Uhtred's Omen" is a real lineage gem; "Omen Sceptre" and "Omen Crest Shield" are bases.
- **Golden Page**: "Golden Charm" is a base (Rite of Passage's). Different words, no clash.
- **Flare**: no item or fake is called Flare; "Redflare" and "Sunflare" appear only inside fake names.
- Clean: Una, Siosa, Gwennen, Renly, Interrogation, Jun, Reveal, Strongbox.

## 9. Aside: "Inscription" is now a PoE2 word

PoE2 0.5 uses it for a Remnant's rune recipe. Liquid Verisium: "Immediately start a Runic Remnant Encounter with a random Runic Inscription" (https://poe2db.tw/us/Liquid_Verisium). This mode calls its tile board the Inscription. The meanings are close (runes in slots, syllables on a line), so it probably passes the rule, but the owner should know the word now has a PoE2 meaning.
