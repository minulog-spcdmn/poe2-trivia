# Wraeclast run: the long tail

Two parts: a boon pool of 30 (the 15 in the plan plus 15 new), and 21 long-tail goals. Everything below works with the rules as the plan writes them. Where a boon bends a rule, the Care column says how far.

## What changes in the tail

| Between runs | Plan now | This draft |
|---|---|---|
| Boon unlocks | about 8, all in the fog | 15: 10 in the fog, 5 from goals |
| Goals for the mode | none | 21: 9 Codex achievements, 12 feats on the map |
| Where the last ones come from | clearing fog (about 5 runs) | tier 5, a flawless run, all 5 boss rules, a clean zone of all 10 types |

The fog still clears in about 5 runs, which is fine: it is the first layer. The goals carry the tail after it, and the hardest of them (League Veteran, Unscathed, the 5 boss seals at tier 5) depend on what the map rolls, so they can't be ground out in a week.

## 1. The boon pool

Start: in the pool from the first run (the plan's 15). Fog: found on a fog node, joins the pool when you win that run (the plan's rule). Goal: joins the pool when its goal is earned, win or not.

### Information

| Lamplighter | The first patch on every item is lifted for free, the boss's items too | Start | Does nothing where the Veil can't be lifted (Delirium, the Blind boss, the Strongbox). Decide whether it adds a patch at the Gambler (it isn't a supply, so by the letter it does). |
| Archivist | Lore reveals are never used up | Start | Dead in Ritual zones, redundant in Incursion. Needs rules: you still need one in the pack (it takes a slot), using it still counts as using something for Clean Hands, and under the Hoarder boss it takes one other supply. |

### Board

| Boon | Effect | From | Care |

| Scribe | The Inscription has 2 fewer names, never below 4 | Start | Blight still has 2 more names than other zones with it, so Blight's rule stands. |

Each hidden zone type has one fog boon. Suggest placing that boon on a different node from where its zone type is found, deeper in the fog, so finding Ultimatum points you to a second search. 10 boons, 4 zone types and 3 shortcuts make 17 secrets on about 40 nodes; put the boons off the most obvious routes.

### Builds

**The Lantern-bearer** (reads the art): Lamplighter, Bright Wick, Gravelight, Afterimage. Routes through Blight and Necropolis for patches, buys only Flares. Made for the Silent and Mirrored bosses, helpless against the Blind boss and in Delirium, so it routes around them.

**The Scholar** (reads the text): Marginalia, Strike Through, Archivist, Waking Dream. Never lifts the Veil; each Scroll gives two lines and clears a name. Loves Ritual (free line plus two), Delirium and the Blind boss. The Silent boss is its nightmare.

**The Bloodletter** (life as gold): Blood Toll, Hearth, Steeled Will, High Roller, Death's Door. Spends the town's heal on supplies and routes into every Ultimatum zone for 2x at normal cost. Runs end on a knife's edge, won or lost.

**The Wayfarer** (route control): Cartographer, Trailblazer, Dowser, Fourfold Deal, Lorekeeper. Picks exactly the zone types and categories it knows and makes its own Well before the boss. The best run for hunting the last fog.

### Considered and left out

- Flares or Name strikes never used up: the pack and the towns stop mattering.
- A free Requirement reveal (or any supply) on every item: that supply stops being bought.
- Towns selling flask charges, or Wells healing life: breaks "only Wells refill", which the plan takes from PoE2.
- Marking the answer's first syllable: too strong, and guests are meant to get syllables unmarked.
- All mirrored patches unmirrored: cancels the Mirrored boss and tiers 4 and 5 (Still Water is scoped to Kalandra for this reason).
- Expedition zones at 1x: the zone would have no rule left.
- The answer's word count as a boon: the plan has it as an open question for a rule; if it becomes one, the boon is dead.

### Names

Checked against items.json and fakes.json: "Blood Price" is a unique helmet, so that boon is Blood Toll; "Sharp Quill" sat too near the unique The Wicked Quill, so it is Second Stroke; "True Reflection" sat too near the made-up name Kalandra's Reflection, so it is Still Water. No new name reuses a currency or item. Trialmaster and Waypoint (in the goals) are a PoE2 NPC and a PoE2 place, used for what they are.

## 2. Long-tail goals

The achievement list is "few and chosen", in four groups of nine so the Codex page lays out evenly. So the goals come in two parts: one new Codex group of nine in the existing style, and twelve feats that live on the mode's map screen, where per-rule and per-type grids belong.

Tiers are the seal metals: lead (very easy), copper, silver, gold. All 29 existing signs are taken, so the new group needs 7 new signs in lib/alchemy.ts; air, water and arsenic already exist as small marks there, and vitriol, libra, leo and sagittarius are classic signs to draw. In co-op, a goal counts for every player still in the party at the end, like a tier unlock.

### Rewards at a glance

- Unlock a boon (5): Seasoned Exile, Defy the Trialmaster, At the Gate, Laid to Rest, Single-minded.
- Unlock a cosmetic (9): Unbowed, Corked, the 5 boss seals, League Veteran, Wraeclast Charted.
- For show (7): Waypoint, Across Wraeclast, Unscathed, Buried with It, Travelling Light, Last Breath, Shared Flask.

### What the run record has to keep

Like lib/delveRecord.ts for Delve, a finished run needs: tier, won or lost, the boss rule, each zone's type and misses, flask drinks, supplies used on boss items, boons held (with kinds), purchases after the camp, pack size at the end, life at the end, and the party size at the end. Everything above can be read from that, or seen as it happens (At the Gate, Buried with It).
