Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They were trimmed, mixed down to mono, loudness-matched and converted to MP3;
the ambience loop is a 30-second excerpt crossfaded into a seamless loop.
How they are layered and filtered is set in src/lib/soundDesign.ts.
